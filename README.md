### Hi, I'm Tammy

I work in customer support and customer success. More recently I have been doing email marketing and crm setup for a marketing agency where I work with clients, and make automations, building AI tools to help me work efficiently and accurately.

Before this I spent 15+ years face to face with customers, nearly a decade of it running a nail salon. Booking schedules crashing, payment processing issues, the wifi going down, I was the person to figure it all out. So when something breaks, I want to fix it and learn from it.

Now I build with Claude. Funnels, webinars, emails and automations in GoHighLevel for a client, and the tools below for my own work. Seeing an automation actually come alive and work is my favorite part.

I speak Vietnamese and English.
